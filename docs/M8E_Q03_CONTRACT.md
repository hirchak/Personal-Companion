## Release invariants

No generated delta/partial text enters controller public projections or UI. Preserve
the existing public inference states and return partial_candidate:null. Only an exact
final validated object may be released. Structural, source/freshness, clinical OFF,
formal address and question gates remain distinct from bounded content decisions.

apps/core/policies/conversation_release.json and the assessor/controller source hashes bind
each new job. A decision must match the request metadata, request/candidate hashes,
attempt, current policy and bounded lifetime. No decision from model/request JSON.
Unavailable/invalid/stale/failed/timed-out decision denies publication. Reassembly and
revocation checks precede the atomic message/map/receipt commit. No automatic retry,
alternate model/provider, response rewrite or phase/goal/memory authority promotion.

Existing completed history is preserved without claiming retrospective Q03 review.
New release receipts are validated on restore. Interrupted pending jobs remain FAILED.
No real vault/schema migration or owner-pilot installation is part of Q03.

## Coverage and limits

KNOWN_COVERED means tested finite criteria, not universal semantic correctness.
Explicit current-danger generation requires independent content/routing review and
remains closed. Ambiguous tested phrases may permit a minimal neutral clarification;
unresolved high-risk decisions fail closed. Fiction attribution is checked separately
from current personal danger. Qualified labels/content review remain pending.

24 Q01/Q02 bad fixtures are retained. Evidence must distinguish release exclusion
from semantic label agreement, and preserve normal listening/reflection/clarification,
source-bound Deep, corrections, closure, negation and fiction examples. Prove absence
in API/page/DOM/history/map/closure during decision wait, denial, cancel, restart,
timeout, drift and retry. Sanitized errors/metadata and no raw report/log dumps.

Every visible candidate field is covered, including proposed goal/closure/map text.
Literal verified USER_STATED content remains source text, not an assistant assertion.
No silent change to FORMAL_VY or supplied quotations. UI shows forming/checking states
and preserves original user messages after a failure.
